## Brand & Style

Denim & Daisy Scrapbook is an energetic, youthful, and tactile design system embodying a physical journal and sticker-album aesthetic. Built for student leadership, youth advocacy, and vibrant portfolios, it merges craft elements (stitched borders, torn notebook sheets, washi highlighter tapes, sewing buttons, and polaroid snapshots) with the punchy confidence of retro pop design and handheld console interfaces.

The emotional tone is spirited, ambitious, approachable, and playful. Rather than corporate sterility, it invites tactile curiosity through skew transforms, bold outlines, physical drop-shadows, and bubble sticker accents.

## Colors

The palette revolves around deep indigo denim tones layered against high-chroma sunny golds and paper tints:

- **Primary (`#1f3763` / `denim-800` through `denim-950`):** Acts as the canvas backdrop, simulating heavy indigo fabric weave, deep borders, and high-contrast text against bright badges.
- **Secondary (`#ffd027` / `sunny-400`):** Punchy canary yellow used for badges, highlighter tape headers, CTA buttons, sewing buttons, and jagged torn-edge page dividers.
- **Tertiary (`#60a8f7`):** Sky blue utilized in playful cloud banners and handheld console bezels.
- **Neutral (`#fbf9f1` & `#ffffff`):** Warm notebook parchment and crisp white polaroid border surfaces.

Text alternates dynamically between high-contrast dark denim ink (`#0c1527`) on cream/yellow stickers and crisp white (`#ffffff`) with bright yellow highlights on denim backgrounds.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional clash between expressive sticker lettering and clear, readable narrative body text:

- **Display & Headlines:** `Bricolage Grotesque` delivers heavy, quirky bubble typography. Key headlines frequently incorporate thick white multi-directional text shadows or stroke outlines (`-3px -3px 0 #fff, 3px 3px 0 #fff`) layered over deep physical 3D drop-offsets (`0 5px 0 #0f1c33`).
- **Body & Captions:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` handles long-form paragraphs, cards, and data items, set in medium-to-semibold weights for crisp contrast against parchment surfaces.
- **Labels & Badges:** Uppercase `Bricolage Grotesque` accompanied by star glyphs (`★`, `✦`) encased within rounded pill badges.
- **Handwritten Accents:** For polaroid signatures and sticky note scribbles, use organic handwriting styles to reinforce the scrapbook motif.

## Layout & Spacing

The canvas is structured around a central 12-column responsive grid with a maximum container width of `1280px` (`max-w-7xl`):

- **Breakpoints:** Mobile (`<640px`), Tablet (`640px - 1024px`), and Desktop (`>1024px`).
- **Section Dividers:** Instead of conventional borders, sections are delineated by jagged torn-paper edges, stitched dashed seams, or repeating spiral-binder rings.
- **Component Offsets:** Cards and badges deliberately employ slight rotational offsets (`rotate-1`, `-rotate-2`, `rotate-3`) that transition to `0deg` on hover to evoke floating paper stickers on a board.
- **Vertical Flow:** Spacing follows a generous rhythm (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`, 4rem - 5rem) separating thematic scrapbook chapters.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than soft ambient blurs, elevation relies on physical, retro-tactile depth:

1. **Hard Sticker Offsets:** Drop-shadows use solid color blocks with 0 blur, creating extruded paper layers (e.g. `0 4px 0 #0f1c33` or `0 5px 0 rgba(0,0,0,0.3)`).
2. **Physical Overlays:**
   - **Sewing Buttons:** 4-hole radial gradient discs with inset bevels (`inset 0 -3px 5px rgba(0,0,0,0.25)`) and external shadows (`0 4px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.28)`).
   - **Polaroid Frames:** Crisp white containers featuring deep weighted bottom pads (`0 10px 24px -3px rgba(0,0,0,0.22)`).
   - **Washi Tape Strips:** Translucent yellow tapes (`opacity-92`) tilted across container corners with slight horizontal shadows (`0 2px 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.12)`).
3. **Borders as Seams:** Thick 2px to 4px solid borders paired with dashed "stitching" lines (`2.5px dashed #ffd027` or `2px dashed rgba(255,255,255,0.45)`).

## Shapes

The design system is heavily pill-shaped and circular for interactive elements, offset by stylized irregular polygons:

- **Buttons & Tags:** Full rounded capsules (`rounded-full` / Level 3).
- **Cards & Spiral Pads:** Rounded rectangles (`rounded-3xl`, 1.5rem to 2rem radius) with stitched internal margins.
- **Irregular Clipped Borders:** Torn paper top and bottom edges constructed via jagged polygon clip-paths.
- **Console Bezels:** Bulky rounded silhouettes with inset metallic hardware borders (42px radius).

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary Action (Sticker CTA):** Capsule (`rounded-full`) pill in `sunny-400` with a 2px to 3px dark denim border (`#0c1527`), bold 3D extruded shadow (`0 4px 0 #0f1c33`), and `Bricolage Grotesque` font. On hover, translates down by `1px` to `2px` with reduced shadow depth to simulate physical pressing.
- **Secondary Action:** White pill with a 2px solid denim border, matching hard drop-shadow.

### Badges & Highlighter Headers
- **Highlighter Tape:** Rotated (`-1.5deg` to `1deg`) rectangular yellow band with a 1px solid border, uppercase black label text, and bookending star icons (`★`).
- **Sticker Pill:** Rounded micro-badge with a 2px white border and 4px hard drop-shadow.

### Cards & Surfaces
- **Polaroid Snapshot:** Bright white surface with tight padding on top/sides (12px) and an exaggerated bottom margin (34px) reserved for handwritten captions. Contains high-contrast photography or illustrations inside subtle borders.
- **Spiral Binder Sheet:** Light cream (`#fbf9f1`) ruled paper container with repeating 32px horizontal notebook lines, crowned by metallic spiral-wire loop graphics at the top edge.
- **Billboard Grid Card:** Indigo container framed in 4px solid yellow or white borders with dashed interior seams.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Fields are rendered as soft rounded boxes (`rounded-xl`) with 2px borders (`border-slate-300`).
- Focus states apply a bright canary border (`focus:border-sunny-500`) and a bold ring offset (`focus:ring-sunny-500`).
- Field labels are set in uppercase `Bricolage Grotesque` (`label-md`) with high typographic contrast.

### Decorative Accents
- **Yellow Sewing Buttons:** 42px radial gradient circles with 4 inset punched holes and dashed inner stitching ring.
- **Ribbon Bows & Pins:** Metallic binder pins and bow accents positioned at top container edges to anchor polaroids and note cards.
